What it does

A budget that keeps up with you

Most budgeting apps tell you what went wrong last month. Settle Down tells you while there's still time to do something about it.

Buckets and alerts

Know where you stand after every purchase

Every card swipe lands in a bucket like Groceries, Dining Out or Gas, and your phone tells you right away how much of that bucket is left.

  • Instant alerts with the bucket and your month-to-date total
  • Start with sensible buckets, then add your own
  • Pace markers show if you're ahead or behind for today, not just the month
  • Split shared bills like rent so only your share counts

Trends and projections

See the month before it happens

Settle Down compares this month to last, then runs your spending forward to show where you're likely to land, with an honest range instead of a single guess.

  • Month-end outlook from thousands of simulated months
  • Bill forecasts so rent and utilities never sneak up on you
  • Budget tune-ups that suggest limits based on how you really spend

SettleDown, your money assistant

Ask your budget anything

"How can I cut back this month?" "What did we spend at Target since summer?" SettleDown answers from your real numbers, in plain English, with the math to back it up.

  • Insights that flag what changed before it becomes a problem
  • A weekly recap of how your week compared to a typical one
  • Automatic sorting for purchases that don't fit an obvious bucket

Savings goals

Savings that stay put

Set aside money for the emergency fund, the vacation or the holidays in their own envelopes, separate from everyday spending. If a bucket runs over, you'll see exactly which goal it's coming out of.

  • Monthly targets with a finish date for every goal
  • Overspending made visible, before it eats your savings

And a few things you'll love

The busywork, handled

Scan a receipt, split the purchase

A Target run is groceries, household and a birthday gift. Snap the receipt and each line goes to the right bucket.

One budget for the household

Invite your partner. Each of you links your own banks, and you both see the same buckets and goals.

Cancel subscriptions for you

Settle Down finds what renews each month. Tap Cancel and SettleDown works through the cancel page while you sign in and approve the final click.

Bills on the calendar

Recurring bills are spotted automatically and forecast ahead, so you know what's still coming this month.

Spending patterns

See which days, stores and habits drive your spending, with lumpy one-off months kept out of the averages.

Find any purchase fast

Filter activity by month, bucket or store and see the total for exactly what you're looking at.

Privacy and security

Your money is personal. We keep it that way.

We built Settle Down for our own family first, so we hold it to the standard we'd want. Read the details in our privacy policy.

We never see your bank password

You sign in to your bank inside Plaid. We get read-only access to balances and transactions, and nothing that can move money.

No ads, no selling

We don't sell your data or use it for advertising. Ever.

Encrypted and locked down

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest, and protected by two-step sign-in.

Leave whenever you like

Unlink a bank or delete your account from Settings, and your data is gone within 30 days.
