Good to know

When will I be charged?

Not today. Your card is charged when the 7-day trial ends, then every month (or every year, if you pick yearly). The app shows how many trial days you have left, and you can cancel any time before then.

How do I cancel?

In the app, go to Settings, then Your plan, then Manage billing, and cancel. Cancel during the trial and you pay nothing; cancel later and you keep access until the end of the period you've paid for.

Does one plan cover my partner?

Yes. One plan covers everyone in your household. Invite them from Settings; they link their own cards and you both see the same buckets and goals.

Is my bank data safe?

You sign in to your bank inside Plaid, so we never see your bank password, and our access is read-only: nothing can move money. Data is encrypted, protected by two-step sign-in, and never sold or used for ads. Payments go through Stripe, so we never see your card number either. Read our privacy policy.

Which banks work?

Most US banks and credit cards, through Plaid. That includes the big national banks, credit unions and card issuers.

Is there an iPhone app?

Settle Down runs today as a web app you add to your iPhone's home screen, and it works on any computer too. The App Store app is on its way, and the same account and plan will carry over.

What are AI credits?

SettleDown, the built-in money assistant, answers questions about your spending, reads receipts to split purchases, and can walk through cancelling a subscription with you. Each plan includes 40 credits a month, which is plenty for most households. Daily insights and the weekly recap don't use credits.

What if it's not for me?

Cancel during the trial and you're never charged. You can also unlink your banks or delete your account from Settings at any time, and your data is deleted. Questions? Email admin@settledownfinancial.com.
